TAMATA (Pvt) Ltd was established in 2023 to manufacture and market a range of food products under the brand TAMATA — for Sri Lankan homes, and for the world.

From that beginning, the name has grown into a quiet mark of excellence: quality products, made for everyday life. A truly Sri Lankan brand, enjoyed by both young and old.

Today TAMATA is becoming a leader in Sri Lanka’s ready-to-eat kitchen. One promise holds it all.
